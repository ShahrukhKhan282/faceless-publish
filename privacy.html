Privacy policy for faceless-publish

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who uses it

faceless-publish is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channels and to read those channels' basic details and video statistics.
  • Facebook and Instagram: permission to publish videos to the owner's own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts and to read their recent posts, so the same video is never posted twice.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a folder readable only by the owner. No data is sent to any server other than Google's, YouTube's and Meta's own services, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and from Facebook's Business integrations settings. Deleting the local token folder also removes it from the tool.
